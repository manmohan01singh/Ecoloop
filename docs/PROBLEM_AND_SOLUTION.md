# EcoLoop - Problem & Solution (Simple Guide)

---

## 🔴 PROBLEM KYA HAI?

### India Mein E-Waste Ka Crisis

**1. Bahut Zyada E-Waste Banta Hai**
- Har saal 17 lakh tonnes e-waste (kharab electronic)
- India duniya mein 3rd largest producer
- 75 crore+ smartphones use ho rahe hain

**2. 80% Kachra Informal Sector Handle Karta Hai**
- Kabadivalle open fire mein cables jalate hain
- Acid se circuit boards saaf karte hain
- Workers bina protection ke kaam karte hain
- Zehreela dhuan hawa mein, chemicals mitti mein

**3. Health Par Bura Asar**
- Bachon ke khoon mein lead level 7 guna zyada (Delhi)
- Workers ko cancer, kidney damage hota hai
- Khana bhi unsafe (sabziyon mein lead 12 guna zyada)

**4. Koi Tracking Nahi**
- Phone kharidne ke baad kahan gaya, kisi ko nahi pata
- Government ko bhi data nahi hai
- Responsible recycling ka koi system nahi

**5. Paise Ka Nuksan**
- Rare metals (gold, silver, cobalt) destroy ho jate hain
- India inhe China se import karta hai 100%
- Hum apne kachre se yeh nikal sakte hain, par nahi nikalte
